Case study · San Josemaría Escrivá School · 2026

Admissions Campaign 2027–2028

StrategyMeta AdsOrganic contentAudiovisual

My role: I designed and ran the full campaign, from diagnosis to ads, creative and measurement.

116conversations with admissions
$0.44per conversation, 48% below the median
197new enrollments in 2025–2026
  1. 01

    Diagnosis

    Around 200 students were leaving the school, between graduates and withdrawals. It runs an afternoon shift and shares its campus with another school that operates in the morning.

    I analyzed the competitor's offer, pricing and messaging to find a distinctive message.

  2. 02

    Strategy

    Audience: parents in the area.
    Message: “Your family, our mission”.
    Channels: organic content and Meta ads that lead straight to the admissions WhatsApp.
    Budget: between $3 and $5 a day.

  3. 03

    Execution

    A six-week organic plan, carousel and video ads, a 6-page brochure with AI-assisted creative direction and the script for a 20-second spot.

    Piece from the 2025–2026 campaign · face blurred
  4. 04

    Results and learnings

    I use Ads Manager data to adjust formats and messages:

    • Carousel vs. video: the same 58 conversations each, but the carousel cost $0.34 per conversation and the video $0.55, 38% less.
    • Who responds: over 80% are women aged 25 to 44, the profile of the mothers who make the enrollment decision.
